Applies to:

This amendment is authoritative and supersedes any conflicting language in the two design documents above.

## 1. The Volunteer Voice has no divisions

Rocky Top Rumble / The Volunteer Voice does **not** use divisions.

Therefore the following prior requirements are removed from the Volunteer Voice contract:

- divisional MVP nominees
- a gold-foil MVP defined by comparing divisional MVP winners
- Division Pulse
- division average scoring
- division head-to-head records
- gaps between divisions
- divisional standings/context
- any other Volunteer Voice output that assumes league divisions exist

The Volunteer Voice remains a six-team family league publication centered on:

- weekly scores and league-median scoring
- official standings/seeding
- Rankings Wire
- Decision Desk / The Call That Won the Week
- lineup efficiency
- Manager of the Week
- weekly MVP / top individual performance
- Benchwarmer
- Rookie of the Week
- Free Agent of the Week
- Bad Beat
- Escape Artist
- Waiver Star
- Bench Blast
- Record Watch
- Chronicle-backed family rivalry and manager-vs-manager history
- next-week scouting context

If a gold-foil MVP treatment is used for the Volunteer Voice, it should represent the **single highest-scoring qualifying started player league-wide**, not a winner selected from nonexistent divisions.

The existing Week 1 paper's `DIVISION PULSE` presentation is not to be treated as a future implementation requirement. Existing published papers are not being regenerated.

## 2. SEC Dynasty / The Saturday Standard retains divisions

The SEC Dynasty league **does** use divisions, and The Saturday Standard must continue to treat those divisions as first-class publication structure.

The following remain locked requirements for The Saturday Standard:

- East and West division identity
- East and West Division Pulse
- division scoring averages and head-to-head records
- East and West Power Polls
- East MVP and West MVP
- gold-foil designation for the higher-scoring divisional MVP
- divisional context in standings, matchup previews, and next-week slate coverage

This amendment does not weaken or alter any of The Saturday Standard's existing offense, IDP, recruiting, transfer-portal, dynasty-market, or future-pick requirements.

## 3. The Stampede feature name is `What a Way to Make a Living`

The Stampede's recurring real-football workload/stat-line feature is officially named:

**WHAT A WAY TO MAKE A LIVING**

This name replaces all prior references to:

- `The Week's Heavy Lifting`
- `Heavy Lifting`

The underlying data requirement is unchanged. The Editorial Desk must preserve real football stat lines, not only fantasy points, so the feature can identify notable weekly workloads and production such as:

- rushing attempts
- rushing yards
- rushing touchdowns
- receiving volume
- receiving yards
- receiving touchdowns
- passing volume, passing yards, and passing touchdowns when editorially useful

The purpose remains to highlight substantial real-world football work and production rather than simply repeat the fantasy-points leaderboard.

## Locked correction summary

Implementation must follow these corrected rules:

- Volunteer Voice: **no divisions**
- Volunteer Voice MVP treatment: league-wide, not divisional
- Saturday Standard: **East/West divisions remain first-class**
- Stampede workload feature: **What a Way to Make a Living**

Any conflicting language in the earlier design checkpoint or newspaper-contract document is superseded by this amendment.
